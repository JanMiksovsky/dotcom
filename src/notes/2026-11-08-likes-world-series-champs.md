<p>This year, the Brewers finally did it, they won it all one week ago. They won playing their way... They dominated the playoffs going 11-2, which included a sweep of the Dodgers, the very team that swept them out of the playoffs last year. The playoffs were tense at times as they kept coming back from behind to win, often by one or two runs... During the playoffs I started to understand some of the underlying aspects of the game, particularly the duel between pitcher and batter. That's the heart of the game to me and as I watched some of the best pitchers in baseball do their thing I saw the Brewers rise to the occasion and have some of the best at bats I've ever seen.</p>
